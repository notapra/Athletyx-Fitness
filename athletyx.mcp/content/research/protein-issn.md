# Protein intake for training — research brief

Keywords: protein, diet, nutrition, muscle, hypertrophy, grams, bodyweight, meal timing

## Key findings (evidence-based)

For most exercising adults building or maintaining muscle, **1.4–2.0 g protein per kg body weight per day** is sufficient (about **0.64–0.91 g per lb**).

Practical coaching ranges often used in IronLog:
- Strength / hypertrophy: aim near the upper end of ISSN range when in a surplus or hard training block.
- Fat loss / cut while lifting: higher protein (often **≥2.3 g/kg** / ~**1.0 g/lb** in literature for lean-mass retention during deficit) can help preserve muscle — still not medical advice.
- Per meal: roughly **20–40 g** high-quality protein, or ~**0.25 g/kg** per serving, spread across the day every **3–4 hours**.

**Timing:** total daily protein matters more than a narrow “anabolic window.” Peri-workout protein can help meet daily totals but is not mandatory if daily intake is already adequate.

## Coach application

Answer protein questions with a clear g/kg (and g/lb) range, note that body weight and goals matter, and remind users Athletyx is not medical nutrition therapy.
