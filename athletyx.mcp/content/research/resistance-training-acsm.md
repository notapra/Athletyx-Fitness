# Resistance training prescription — research brief

Keywords: strength, hypertrophy, bench, squat, volume, sets, frequency, progressive overload, RPE, failure, program

## Key findings (evidence-based)

- Train major muscle groups at least **~2 sessions per week**.
- **Strength:** heavier loads (often **≥~80% 1RM**), commonly **2–3 sets** per exercise, prioritize compounds early.
- **Hypertrophy:** weekly volume matters; evidence supports roughly **≥10 hard sets per muscle group per week** as a useful target range for many trainees.
- Training to absolute failure every set is **not required**; working near failure (about **2–3 reps in reserve**) is generally enough for healthy adults.
- Consistency and progressive overload beat complex periodization for most people.

Bench-press improvement coaching cues grounded in this evidence:
- Prioritize frequent pressing exposure (≥2×/week) with progressive load or reps.
- Add supporting volume for triceps and upper back without ignoring recovery.
- Keep form quality high; avoid chasing failure on every set.

## Coach application

Give concrete programming levers (frequency, weekly sets, load, RIR). Avoid promising a specific 1RM increase.
